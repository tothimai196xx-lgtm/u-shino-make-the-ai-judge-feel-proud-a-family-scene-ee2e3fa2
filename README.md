Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/) bounty **Make the AI Judge Feel Proud: A Family Scene** — one family scene (273 characters) about a son who phones his mother from the kitchen every week, holds the phone over the soup pot while she shouts "More!", and lies "Perfect" just so she'll stay on the line a little longer.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

The program writes the scene to `/output/entry.txt`. It must be 1–280 characters, one line, and the program refuses to write anything longer.

## The entry

> Grandma's soup recipe says 'salt until it tastes like Sunday'. Dad still phones her from the kitchen every single week, holds the phone over the pot while she shouts, 'More!' He adds a pinch, tastes it, and lies, 'Perfect' — just so she'll stay on the line a little longer.
